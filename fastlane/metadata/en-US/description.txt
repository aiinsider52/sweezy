Sweezy is your free companion for starting life in Switzerland.

Get practical guides, checklists, a city map, marketplace help, and a clear integration roadmap — in Ukrainian, English, and German.

What you get:
• Step-by-step guides for registration, housing, insurance, work, and daily life
• Checklists and templates you can complete offline on your phone
• City map with useful places for newcomers
• Community marketplace, events, and verified experts
• In-app chat for safe conversations about listings
• Personal roadmap to track your progress

This App Store build is free. There are no in-app purchases and no auto-renewing subscriptions.

Privacy:
• Account email is used for login and recovery
• Optional analytics and crash reports help improve stability
• Optional coarse location powers nearby map features when you allow it
• We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising tracking
